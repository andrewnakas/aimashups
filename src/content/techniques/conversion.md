---
name: Game conversion
summary: One game's worlds, assets or mechanics are converted into another game, which then runs on its own; the source game isn't running alongside.
order: 3.7
---
A conversion doesn't link two running games. A tool reads one game's data from the player's own copy, such as its maps, models or block textures, and rebuilds it inside another game, which then plays it natively. [TamrielMerge](/mashups/tamrielmerge/) converts older Elder Scrolls games into Skyrim this way, and [GrandTheftMinecraft](/mashups/grand-theft-minecraft/) recreates Minecraft's blocks inside GTA without Minecraft running. As with the other techniques, the converted data stays on the player's machine and isn't redistributed.
