Many of the most-viewed mashup clips were never released. Without code or a build, there's no way to tell whether a clip shows a working passthrough mod, a rewrite, a video composite, or something in between. Coverage at the time noted that a large share of the viral clips were unverified and not downloadable.

This site lists these entries for the historical record, marks them **video only** or **unreleased**, and updates them if code appears.
