Rustify turns Skyrim's whole map into Minecraft blocks. Its video description says the result matches Skyrim "block for block", with Minecraft colours all the way to the horizon, while Skyrim's NPCs, quests and dragons stay in place. It runs on top of [SkyCraft](/mashups/skycraft-minecraft-in-skyrim/), which supplies the Minecraft player, physics, inventory and blocks inside Skyrim.

The conversion runs on your own PC from your own game files, so nothing from either game is uploaded. You need Skyrim Special Edition or Anniversary Edition, SKSE, SkyCraft and a Minecraft Java account. The creator calls it an early alpha.

It went up on Nexus Mods with a one-take showcase video on 10 October 2026. The creator hasn't said how it was made, so it's marked unverified.
