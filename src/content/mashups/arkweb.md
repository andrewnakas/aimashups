ArkWeb puts Spider-Man's web-swinging into Gotham. It runs both games at once and needs a PC that can handle both, a controller, and Spider-Man Remastered at one specific version.

Its install process is a sign of the times. There's no installer. The README tells you to open Claude Code in an empty folder and paste one message, and then the agent downloads the repo, follows its `AGENTS.md`, checks your game versions, builds the mod, backs up your save and walks you through your first session. The **agent is the installer**.
