This mashup combines two kinds of reimplementation: the long-running **reVC** and **re3** reverse-engineered sources for Vice City and GTA III, and the new **Skate 3 Rust rewrite**. The result is skating, board throws and tricks through Vice City's streets, with San Andreas promised next.

Earlier coverage listed it as "Skateboarding in Vice City". The repository was later renamed to cover the trilogy.
