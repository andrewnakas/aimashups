On 12 January 2026 notCHase posted a Ship of Harkinian mod that puts the physics winch from RV There Yet?, Nuggets Entertainment's 2025 co-op road-trip game, on Epona. A follow-up Short used it on Mido as a piñata. No AI tool is credited, so it's listed as a [conventional mod](/techniques/conventional-mod/). It hasn't been released. See also notCHase's [Pikmin in Ocarina of Time](/mashups/pikmin-in-ocarina-of-time/).
