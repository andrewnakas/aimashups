HelloCraft combines Minecraft with Hello Neighbor, tinyBuild's stealth horror game about breaking into the house across the street. A real Minecraft client runs hidden. Hello Neighbor (with UE4SS) shows what it renders and tells it where the walls are, and Minecraft tells Hello Neighbor where the player is and what they're doing. You get Minecraft movement (elytra and ender pearls included), blocks on the neighbour's floors, water and lava, and mobs to set on him. Hello Neighbor's own game keeps working too: doors, picking up and throwing its objects, hiding in cupboards, being caught. **F4** hands movement back to Hello Neighbor, which the README says you need for the finale boss fight.

The README credits [SkyCraft](/mashups/skycraft-minecraft-in-skyrim/) for the approach, and some of its components are adapted from it. The initial release commit is co-authored by Claude Sonnet 5.5. Version 1.0, the only release, came out on 6 October 2026 and had nearly 2,700 downloads by 13 October.
