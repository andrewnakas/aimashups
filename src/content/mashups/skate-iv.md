SkateIV brings the Skate 3 ports to Liberty City, after [Bully](/mashups/bullyskate/), [San Andreas](/mashups/gta-san-anskateas/), [GTA III and Vice City](/mashups/gta-skate-3-trilogy/) and [GTA V](/mashups/sk8v/). GTA IV keeps the world, player model, clothing, lighting and traffic, and the Skate 3 Rust engine supplies the skating physics and animations.

MaybeJason posted it in the chasm Discord on 10 October 2026 as a work in progress that "needs some love". The v0.8.11 preview needs GTA IV **1.0.8.0** (the current Steam Complete Edition executable isn't supported) and your own extracted Xbox 360 Skate 3 files, and setup converts everything locally. Collision, grind transfers, shadows and performance still have known issues. The repository doesn't say whether an AI agent was used, so it's marked unverified.
