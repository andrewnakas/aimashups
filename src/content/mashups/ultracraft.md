ULTRACRAFT is a passthrough mod in the SkyCraft mould, but pointed the other way: Minecraft is the host world, and ULTRAKILL runs alongside it through a BepInEx 5 plugin called UltraBridge. Press **F8** to switch between Steve and V1, ULTRAKILL's robot, and take its movement and guns into a block world.

Its commits credit Claude Opus 5.5 as co-author. It's Windows only and needs ULTRAKILL on Steam. The release comes as a Modrinth `.mrpack` or CurseForge zip that bundles Fabric API, Sodium and Essential, or you can install it on Fabric 1.21.11 by hand. With more than 5,600 release downloads by 9 October 2026, it's one of the most-downloaded mashups since the first wave.

The GitHub repository was taken down in October 2026 and the mod is no longer available from its creator.
