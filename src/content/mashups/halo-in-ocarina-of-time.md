On 6 March 2025 notCHase posted "a collection of mods i made" for Ship of Harkinian, the open-source PC port of Ocarina of Time, built around Halo. Link drives a Warthog across Hyrule, throws grenades and swings an Energy Sword; Retro Dodo noted a sequence where the sword goes through a portal before the Warthog lands in Lake Hylia. A follow-up Short gave the Energy Sword unlimited range.

It's a [conventional mod](/techniques/conventional-mod/) and, like the rest of notCHase's crossovers (including [Pikmin in Ocarina of Time](/mashups/pikmin-in-ocarina-of-time/)), it has only been shown on video.
