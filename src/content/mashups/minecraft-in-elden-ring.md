"Minecraft in Elden Ring" was the headline example in nearly every article about the trend, and the clip most people saw first. VGTimes reported about 22.5 million views on a clip by Tobyn Jacobs, posted around 29 September 2026, and described it as a Mac-only build with no code released.

It's also why the trend drew so much skepticism. With the clip spreading faster than any download, it became the example critics pointed to when they called the wave engagement-farming. VGC quoted modders who called the trend "slop", and Kotaku ran a plea for modders to stop. Playable projects like [SkyCraft](/mashups/skycraft-minecraft-in-skyrim/) had to work to separate themselves from it.
