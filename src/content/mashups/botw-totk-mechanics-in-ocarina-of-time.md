Before his cross-franchise mods took off, notCHase spent late 2023 and most of 2024 demaking Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom into Ocarina of Time. Each one is a mod he made for Ship of Harkinian, the open-source PC port, and posted to YouTube:

|---|---|---|---|

| 17 Dec 2023 | [If Link had Ultrahand in Ocarina of Time](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=45DS0m_F118) | Ultrahand (TotK) | 146,259 |

Ultrahand and Fuse got the most attention, with Nintendo Life covering both. The shield-surfing video plays it as Tony Hawk's Pro Skater. His Smash Bros. jumps video (December 2023) also adds Revali's Gale; it's listed under [Smash Bros. moves in Ocarina of Time](/mashups/smash-bros-in-ocarina-of-time/).

These all predate the AI mashup wave and are [conventional mods](/techniques/conventional-mod/). None has been released. They led to his cross-franchise work, including [Pikmin in Ocarina of Time](/mashups/pikmin-in-ocarina-of-time/).
