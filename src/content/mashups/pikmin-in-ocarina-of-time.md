notCHase posted "If Pikmin were in Ocarina of Time" on YouTube on 22 September 2024, describing it as "a mod i made" for Ship of Harkinian, the open-source PC port of Ocarina of Time. Link plucks Pikmin seeds in Hyrule Field and uses them to kill Deku Babas from a distance, carry boulders, repair the bridge to Gerudo Valley and get thrown up to pieces of heart. Each colour keeps its weakness, so red Pikmin die in Lake Hylia and blue ones on Death Mountain. Time Extension and Zelda Dungeon both covered it.

It came back in October 2026, during the AI mashup wave. [@_gmedley's Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeSRsiui3E2/) compiled Ocarina of Time mods "from 2024 BVC (before vibe coding)", ending on this one, with a caption asking anyone who knows notCHase to get him to release it. It's a [conventional mod](/techniques/conventional-mod/), one of a run of crossovers notCHase has built in Ship of Harkinian since 2023, including [Halo](/mashups/halo-in-ocarina-of-time/), [Samus's arm cannon](/mashups/samus-arm-cannon-in-ocarina-of-time/) and [Smash Bros. moves](/mashups/smash-bros-in-ocarina-of-time/). None of them has been released.
