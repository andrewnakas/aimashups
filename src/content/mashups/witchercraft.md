WitcherCraft is a port of [SkyCraft](/mashups/skycraft-minecraft-in-skyrim/) to The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt (patch 5.0/5.01). A `dinput8.dll` plugin and WitcherScript files on the Witcher side talk to a Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3 through shared memory. Minecraft runs hidden and drives the player, inventory, blocks and combat, and the Witcher keeps its world, people, monsters and quests. **F8** switches between Steve and Geralt. As Steve you can still use Witcher senses (V), call a horse (X) and open the map, journal and inventory.

Steve's blocks live in one Minecraft world, so a house built in Velen also stands at the same spot in Skellige and Toussaint. Only Steve sees them: Geralt and the Witcher's NPCs walk through. You save the game as Geralt.

The release zip bundles a portable Prism Launcher with a ready instance, so you don't need the official Minecraft Launcher, only an account that owns Java Edition. Version 0.1.0 came out on 11 October 2026. Its only commit is co-authored by Claude Opus 5.5 and calls the project a "Port of SkyCraft (MIT) by chasmlol".
