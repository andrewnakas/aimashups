An ASI mod for classic GTA San Andreas, written in C++ with plugin-sdk, that plays Minecraft and San Andreas together: mine and build in Los Santos, craft, survive, fight Creepers and the Warden, dig into the map with TNT, and still drive cars. Its commits credit Claude as co-author. The first packaged release, v0.51, came out on 9 October 2026. It needs the PC version 1.0 US `gta_sa.exe`. The repository has no licence yet.
