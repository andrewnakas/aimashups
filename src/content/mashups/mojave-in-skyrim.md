Mojave in Skyrim replaces Skyrim's music with Fallout: New Vegas's. Skyrim still decides what a moment needs (wilderness, town, dungeon, combat, boss, discovery, death), and the plugin answers with New Vegas's matching tracks: desert exploration in the wilds, Freeside in towns, vault music underground, battle loops in fights, with day and night versions on Skyrim's clock. **N** switches on Radio New Vegas, with its songs, Mr. New Vegas's introductions and the news. Story moments keep Skyrim's own music.

Every song and line is read from your own New Vegas install while Skyrim runs, and nothing is shipped. It's a CommonLibSSE-NG plugin loaded as `MojaveInSkyrim.asi` (no SKSE needed), built for [Melty](https://melty.gg) installs; the repository has the source but no release. Its commits are co-authored by Claude Opus 5.5.
