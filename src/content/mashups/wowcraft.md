WoWCraft runs Minecraft inside a World of Warcraft 1.12 client. A launcher, `WowCraft.exe`, asks for your WoW 1.12 folder and uses Prism Launcher for the Minecraft sign-in, then the two run together: C brings up WoW's cursor, M, L, B and O open WoW's panels, and E and T are Minecraft's inventory and chat.

The creator's demo video went up on 1 October 2026 and had passed 210,000 views on YouTube by 9 October; the repository followed on 5 October. Neither the README nor the commits say whether an AI agent was used, so it's marked unverified. It targets the 1.12 client, which today is only playable on private servers.
