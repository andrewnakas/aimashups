San AnSkateas brings Skate 3's skating to San Andreas v1.0. The game side is a C++ ASI plugin (`SanAnskateas.asi`) built on plugin-sdk; the skating comes from [skate-3-rust-engine](https://github.com/SK8-ENGINE/skate-3-rust-engine). It's one of four mashups that reused that engine in the first week, alongside the [2010 Rust Rewrite Mashup](/mashups/2010-rust-rewrite-mashup/), [BullySkate](/mashups/bullyskate/) and the [GTA Skate 3 Trilogy](/mashups/gta-skate-3-trilogy/).
