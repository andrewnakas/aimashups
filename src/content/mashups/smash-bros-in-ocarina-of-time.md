Between December 2023 and January 2024 notCHase posted three Ship of Harkinian mods that bring Super Smash Bros. into Ocarina of Time. On 5 December 2023 Link got his Smash Bros. jumps (alongside Revali's Gale from Breath of the Wild). On 1 January 2024 "If Link was Fox from SSBM" gave him Fox's moves from Melee; Retro Dodo described a Star Fox Mask that grants the reflector. On 18 January "If Link could FALCON PUNCH in Ocarina of Time" let him Falcon Punch NPCs like Mido and King Zora. At over 250,000 views, the Falcon Punch video is one of notCHase's most watched.

All three are [conventional mods](/techniques/conventional-mod/), shown on video and never released. notCHase's later crossovers include [Pikmin](/mashups/pikmin-in-ocarina-of-time/) and [Halo](/mashups/halo-in-ocarina-of-time/).
