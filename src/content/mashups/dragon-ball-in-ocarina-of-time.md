On 24 April 2026 notCHase posted "Ocarina of Time, but it's Dragon Ball". In his words: "i put Kamehameha and the Flying Nimbus from Dragon Ball into the The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time". Like his other mods, it runs in Ship of Harkinian, the open-source PC port. The moves come from the Dragon Ball anime and manga rather than from a particular Dragon Ball game. At over 640,000 views, it's the most-watched video on his channel.

It was posted five months before the AI mashup wave and credits no AI tool, so it's listed as a [conventional mod](/techniques/conventional-mod/). It hasn't been released. See also notCHase's [Pikmin in Ocarina of Time](/mashups/pikmin-in-ocarina-of-time/).
