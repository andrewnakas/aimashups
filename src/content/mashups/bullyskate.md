BullySkate runs the [Skate 3 Rust rewrite](/techniques/rust-rewrite/) inside Bully: Scholarship Edition, so Jimmy can push, flip and grind around Bullworth. It loads through Derpy's Script Loader and the Ultimate ASI Loader, has controller support and its own rigging, and needs your own game files.

The skate audio is a port by Hailey Ross built on the reverse-engineering of Skate 3's RenderWare Audio in [skate3-audio](https://github.com/andrewnakas/skate3-audio).
