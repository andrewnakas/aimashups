Amazing Frog Skate puts the frog on a Skate 3 board. Press **J** or click both thumbsticks and you're skating, with flick-it tricks, manuals, grinds, trick scoring, session markers and a replay editor. There's a custom skate park in Test Rooms, and guns from the Phone-G Guns menu can be aimed and fired while you ride.

The GitHub repository holds only a README, and the mod ships as a Windows installer on its releases page. The installer's third-party notice is [SkateGM](/mashups/skategm/)'s, which credits its skate engine to the `skate/` engine of the [2010 Rust Rewrite Mashup](/mashups/2010-rust-rewrite-mashup/), whose crates come from skate-3-rust-engine. Amazing Frog Skate adds Unity retargeting, bridge code, camera, UI and replay features, plus its own grind and scoring changes. Setup makes a separate copy of Amazing Frog Legacy, applies hash-checked patches and prepares animations, boards and audio from your own Skate 3 Xbox 360 files. Neither original game is changed.

SLUSCH released v1.0 and v1.1 on 12 October 2026 and calls it experimental: weapon poses, board-carry animations and some interactions may still look or behave wrong. Nothing in the repository or the installer says whether an AI agent was used, so it's marked unverified.
