notCHase posted "If Link had Samus's Arm Cannon in OOT" on 24 December 2023, describing it as "a collection of mods i made" for Ship of Harkinian, the open-source PC port of Ocarina of Time. Link gets Samus's arm cannon from Metroid, and Retro Dodo described its charge beam blowing enemies back. It's one of notCHase's earliest crossovers, posted in the same month as his [Smash Bros. moves](/mashups/smash-bros-in-ocarina-of-time/). It's a [conventional mod](/techniques/conventional-mod/) and hasn't been released.
