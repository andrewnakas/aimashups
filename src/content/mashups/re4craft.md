RE4Craft (repository name ResidentCraft4) runs a hidden Minecraft 26.3 client alongside the original 32-bit Resident Evil 4 UHD on Steam. An ASI plugin draws Minecraft's player meshes, hands, HUD and inventory into RE4. Minecraft handles movement, physics, blocks and combat, and RE4 keeps its terrain, enemies and campaign scripts. Press **F8** to switch into Minecraft mode. Enemies and crates drop Minecraft supplies matched to the chapter, from wood in chapter 1 up to netherite upgrade materials in chapter 5.

Its README says it was inspired by [SkyCraft](/mashups/skycraft-minecraft-in-skyrim/) v0.1.0, and the Minecraft exporter is adapted from it. It also credits OpenAI Codex and [universal-modder](/mashups/minecraft-in-gta-v/) playbooks for the development. The README describes the project as "vibe-coded" and in a very early stage. The single release, 0.2.0-alpha.2, came out on 11 October 2026. Its controls keep an AZERTY layout (ZQSD to move, X for the inventory).
