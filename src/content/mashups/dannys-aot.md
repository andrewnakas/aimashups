Danny's AOT isn't part of the AI wave: it's a hand-made Minecraft mod that has been updated since December 2025, and it's here because it circulated alongside the mashup clips. It adds Attack on Titan's ODM gear (G and H to grapple, Space to boost, R to reload blades), Titans and shifter abilities, with kronart credited for the models. Version 2.5.0, the "Cart Titan / ODM Rework", came out on 2 October 2026.

It runs on Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric, Fabric API, GeckoLib and AAA Particles. The download figure combines CurseForge (about 679,000) and Modrinth (about 329,000) as shown on 9 October 2026.
