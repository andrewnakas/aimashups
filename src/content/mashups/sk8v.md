SK8V brings Skate 3's core skating mechanics to Los Santos in GTA V Legacy, joining the Skate 3 ports to [Bully](/mashups/bullyskate/), [San Andreas](/mashups/gta-san-anskateas/) and [GTA III and Vice City](/mashups/gta-skate-3-trilogy/). Its NOTICE file credits the skate engine from the [2010 Rust Rewrite Mashup](/mashups/2010-rust-rewrite-mashup/) (itself a fork of IW4L) and a live GTA collision reader adapted from Sol4ra's LS-Skate-LiveCollision. It loads through ScriptHookV, which it fetches from the official site rather than bundling. The repository doesn't say whether an AI agent was used, so it's marked unverified.
