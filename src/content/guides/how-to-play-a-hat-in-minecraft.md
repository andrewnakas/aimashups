[A Hat in Minecraft](/mashups/a-hat-in-minecraft/) lets you play Minecraft: Java Edition as Hat Kid, with A Hat in Time running in the background. This guide follows the release notes on [chazbrooksy/A-Hat-in-Time-X-Minecraft](https://github.com/chazbrooksy/A-Hat-in-Time-X-Minecraft/releases); the repository has no README. New to mods? Read [Start here](/guides/start-here-playing-game-mashups/) first.

## 1. What you need
- **Windows**.
- **Minecraft: Java Edition**, owned, on a Microsoft account.
- **A Hat in Time** installed on Steam. The Vanessa's Curse outfits only show if you own that DLC.

No game files are included: Minecraft is downloaded with your own account, and Hat Kid is loaded from your own A Hat in Time install.

## 2. Install
1. Download the `A-Hat-in-Minecraft-<version>.zip` from the [release](https://github.com/chazbrooksy/A-Hat-in-Time-X-Minecraft/releases) and unzip it anywhere.
2. Double-click **A Hat in Minecraft**.
3. Log in with your Microsoft account in Prism Launcher. The first launch downloads Minecraft, Fabric and Java, which takes a couple of minutes.
4. A Hat in Time starts in the background. Play once the menu says Hat Kid is ready.

## 3. What you get
- Hat Kid's real movement, model, animations and sounds: she runs, jumps, dives and homing-attacks as in her own game.
- All five hats with their abilities, plus badges, dyes and outfits.
- The umbrella attacks, health pons and A Hat in Time's health meter.
- Controller support with A Hat in Time's layout.
